# `keypress-ldr` setup

## Hand and keyboard

- Mount the Wuji Hand 2 upright on a fixed table stand.
- Position the index, middle, and ring fingertips over the centers of LEFT, DOWN, and RIGHT, respectively. Keep each fingertip within 2 mm of its key center.
- Set the rest pose 1 cm above the key surface, with no key pressed.
- Use a dedicated USB keyboard read by evdev. Clamp or tape it to the table so it cannot move.

## Camera

Place the D435 40 cm in front of the keyboard and 40 cm above the table. Aim it down at 45 degrees toward the middle key.

See the [`keypress-ldr` protocol](../SPEC.md#keypress-ldr) for trial and scoring rules.
